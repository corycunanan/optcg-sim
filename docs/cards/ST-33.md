# ST-33

## Koby
**ST33-001** · Character · Blue

[Blocker] (After your opponent declares an attack, you may rest this card to make it the new target of the attack.)
[On Play] You may trash 1 card from your hand: Draw 1 card.

---

## Sakazuki
**ST33-002** · Character · Blue

[When Attacking] You may trash 1 card from your hand: If your opponent has 6 or more cards in their hand, your opponent trashes 1 card from their hand.
[On K.O.] Play up to 1 {Navy} type Character card with a cost of 4 or less from your hand.

---

## Smoker
**ST33-003** · Character · Blue

[On Play] You may trash 1 card from your hand: Place up to 2 of your opponent's Characters with a cost of 2 or less at the bottom of the owner's deck.

---

## Borsalino
**ST33-004** · Character · Blue

During the turn in which a card in your hand is trashed by an effect, give this card in your hand −3 cost.
[Blocker] (After your opponent declares an attack, you may rest this card to make it the new target of the attack.)

---

## Monkey.D.Garp
**ST33-005** · Character · Blue

[On Play] If your Leader has the {Navy} type, play up to 1 blue {Navy} type Character card with 8000 power or less other than [Monkey.D.Garp] from your hand.
