# ST-32

## Kin'emon
**ST32-001** · Character · Green

[On Play] You may rest your Slash attribute Leader or 1 of your DON!! cards: Draw 2 cards and trash 1 card from your hand.

---

## Kouzuki Oden
**ST32-002** · Character · Green

[On Play] Draw 1 card, and up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with a base cost of 6 or less cannot be rested until the end of your opponent's next End Phase.

## Dracule Mihawk
**ST32-003** · Character · Green

[Your Turn] When this Character becomes rested, draw 1 card and trash 1 card from your hand.
[On Play] If your Leader has the Slash attribute, play up to 1 Character card with a cost of 5 or less that is either [Perona] or has the Slash attribute from your hand.
