# The Dominance of God [OP-18]


## OP18-001 — Karoo

**Q:** If 2 of my {Alabasta} type Characters would be K.O.'d simultaneously by an opponent's effect, can I draw 2 cards using this [Once Per Turn] effect?

**A:** No, you cannot. In this case, you still only draw 1 card.


## OP18-004 — Karoo

**Q:** If this Character has 7000 power or more, does it gain [Rush], [Banish], and [Unblockable]?

**A:** Yes, in this case the Character gains all 3 abilities: [Rush], [Banish], and [Unblockable].


## OP18-005 — Kung Fu Jugon

**Q:** If this card itself is the only copy of this Character I have on my field, will this Character still gain +1000 power?

**A:** Yes, it will still gain +1000 power.

**Q:** If my Leader has an effect that reads "is treated as a card with all card names, types, and attributes," can I count this Leader as [Kung Fu Jugon] for the purpose of this Character's [Your Turn] effect?

**A:** Yes, you can. In this case, this Character gains +1000 power for every [Kung Fu Jugon] including your Leader.


## OP18-006 — Koza

**Q:** If my Leader has an effect that reads "is treated as a card with all card names, types, and attributes," will this Character gain [Rush] and +1000 power?

**A:** Yes, it will.


## OP18-015 — Eyelashes

**Q:** If my Leader has the {Alabasta} type, can I use this [On Your Opponent's Attack] effect to give one of my opponent's Leader or Characters −3000 power during this turn, even if my target wasn't the one that attacked me?

**A:** Yes, you can.


## OP18-017 — Roronoa Zoro

**Q:** If this Character itself would be removed from the field by my opponent's effect, can I still use this [Once Per Turn] effect to give my Leader −2000 during this turn instead?

**A:** Yes, you can.


## OP18-018 — Let's Go, Karoo!!

**Q:** After activating and resolving this [Main] effect, if I play any other {Alabasta} type Characters during this turn, will they also gain +1000 power during this turn?

**A:** No, they will not.


## OP18-019 — Quack!!!

**Q:** If I have 0 Characters, can I still use this [Main] effect to give up to 1 of my opponent's Characters −3000 power during this turn?

**A:** Yes, you can.


## OP18-020 — Stop Fighting!!!

**Q:** If I have 0 Life cards, can I still use this [Counter] effect to give my opponent's Leader and all of their Characters −5000 power during this turn?

**A:** No, you cannot. This effect only allows you to add all your Life cards to your hand and give your opponent's Leader and all of their Characters −5000 power during this turn if you have at least 1 Life card.


## OP18-021 — Franky

**Q:** What does "+3000 Counter" mean here?

**A:** This effect allows you to use a Stage card from your hand during the Counter step, just like you would a Character card with a [Counter +1000] or [Counter +2000] effect, to give your Leader or one of your Characters +3000 power.


## OP18-022 — Monkey.D.Luffy

**Q:** If I set this Leader as active using its [When Attacking] effect and it is still active when my next turn begins, what happens with this Leader during that turn's Refresh Phase?

**A:** In this case, your Leader will stay active.


## OP18-026 — Sanji

## OP18-031 — Nico Robin

**Q:** If this Character is already rested and one of my Characters would be removed from the field by my opponent's effect, can I use this Character's [Opponent's Turn] effect to rest this Character instead?

**Q:** Can I use this card's [End of Your Turn] effect to set 1 of my opponent's DON!! cards as active?

## OP18-034 — Franky

## OP18-037 — Yokozuna

**Q:** Can I use this [On K.O.] effect to rest one of my opponent's cards that isn't a Character card?

**A:** This effect allows you to rest 1 Character, Stage, Leader, or DON!! card on your opponent's field.


## OP18-041 — Ms. All Sunday

**Q:** If my opponent has 0 cards in their hand, can I still use this Leader's effect to draw 1 card when my Character with a type including "Baroque Works" and 3000 base power or more is K.O.'d?

**A:** Yes, you can. In this case, this effect has no effect on your opponent.

**Q:** Can I refuse to draw a card when using this Leader's effect?

**A:** No, you cannot. When your Character with a type including "Baroque Works" and 3000 base power or more is K.O.'d, you must draw 1 card and have your opponent trash 1 card from their hand.

**Q:** Which player chooses what card is trashed from the opponent's hand when this Leader's effect is activated?

**A:** The opponent of the player who used this effect will choose 1 card to trash from their hand.


## OP18-046 — Mr.0 & Ms. All Sunday

**Q:** Can I use this [On Play] effect to only give 1 DON!! card to my Leader or one of my Characters, or choose not to give any rested DON!! cards at all?

**A:** Yes, you can. This effect allows you to choose to give anywhere between 0 to 2 rested DON!! cards to your Leader and each of your individual Characters.


## OP18-051 — Mr.4 & Miss.MerryChristmas

**Q:** Can I use this [On Play] effect to put 1 of my Characters and 1 of my opponent's Characters at the bottom of their owner's decks?

**A:** Yes, you can. This effect allows you to place up to 1 Character with a cost of 6 or less and up to 1 Character with a cost of 5 or less at the bottom of the owner's deck, regardless of who they belong to.
