# ST-35 FAQ


## ST35-003 — Karasu

**Q:** Which player chooses what cards are trashed when this card's [When Attacking] effect is activated?

**A:** The opponent of the player who used this [When Attacking] effect will choose 1 card to trash from their hand.

**Q:** If my opponent has 6 or less cards in their hand, can I still use this card's [When Attacking] effect to trash 2 cards from the top of my deck?

**A:** Yes, you can. In this case, since your opponent does not have 7 or more cards in their hand, your opponent will not trash any cards from their hand.
