## ST30-001 — Luffy & Ace

**Q:** If I have 0 Character cards, does it mean this Leader gets −2000 power?

**A:** No, it doesn't.

**Q:** Can I use this [Opponent's Turn] effect to give this Leader +3000 power?

**A:** No, you cannot.
