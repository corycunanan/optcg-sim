# One Piece Heroines Edition vol.2 [EB-05]


## EB05-006 — Miss Buckingham Stussy

**Q:** Can I use this [Activate: Main] effect to give −4000 to one of my Characters or +3000 to one of my opponent's Characters?

**A:** Yes, you can.

**Q:** When using this [Activate: Main] effect, can I look at the card I take from the top of my Life cards before choosing which of the two effects I'd like to apply?

**A:** Yes, you can.

## EB05-010 — Nico Robin

**Q:** Say my "EB01-057 Shirahoshi" with the <Wisdom> attribute and that originally had [Blocker] was K.O.'d after its effect was negated through the [Main] effect of "OP09-098 Black Hole." In this situation, can I use this Leader's [Once Per Turn] effect to add up to 1 card from the top of my deck to the top of my Life cards?

**A:** Yes, you can. If a Character that originally has [Blocker] is affected by an effect that states "negate the effect", its [Blocker] effect gets negated, so the Character does not have [Blocker] during that time.

**Q:** Say my "EB04-057 Vegapunk", to which I gave DON!!x1, and has the <Wisdom> attribute and—thanks to its ongoing effect—[Blocker], was K.O.'d after its effect was negated through the [Main] effect of "OP09-098 Black Hole." In this situation, can I use my Leader's [Once Per Turn] effect to add up to 1 card from the top of my deck to the top of my Life cards?

**A:** Yes, you can.

**Q:** Say my {Egghead} type Character with the <Wisdom> attribute gained [Blocker] thanks to the [Trigger] effect of "OP07-103 Tony Tony.Chopper". During this turn, if that Character that gained [Blocker] then gets its [Blocker] effect negated by the effect of my opponent's "OP09-098 Black Hole" and is K.O.'d, can I use this Leader's [Once Per Turn] effect to add up to 1 card from the top of my deck to the top of my Life cards?

**A:** No, you cannot. In this situation, the [Blocker] gained through the [Trigger] effect of "OP07-103 Tony Tony.Chopper" cannot be negated by effects that state "negate the effect".
