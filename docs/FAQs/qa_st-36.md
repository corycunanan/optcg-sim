# ST-36 FAQ


## ST36-001 — Cavendish

**Q:** If I have no cards in my hand, can I use this [On K.O.] effect to add 1 card from the top of my deck to the top of my Life cards without trashing 1 card from my hand?

**A:** No, you cannot.


## ST36-002 — Killer

**Q:** Can I activate this [On Play] effect during my opponent's turn?

**A:** No, you cannot.


## ST36-003 — Scratchmen Apoo

**Q:** Can I still use this [Trigger] effect to draw 1 card even if my Leader doesn't have the {Supernovas} type?

**A:** Yes, you can. In this case, you will be unable to change your Leader's base power to 7000 after drawing 1 card.


## ST36-004 — Bartolomeo

**Q:** If I have no {Supernovas} type cards in my hand, can I still use this [On Play] effect to draw 2 cards?

**A:** No, you cannot.


## ST36-005 — Eustass"Captain"Kid

**Q:** Can I still activate this [On Your Opponent's Attack] effect if both the top and bottom cards of my Life cards are already face-down?

**A:** No, you cannot.

**Q:** Can I still activate this [Activate: Main] effect if both the top and bottom cards of my Life cards are already face-up?

**A:** No, you cannot.
