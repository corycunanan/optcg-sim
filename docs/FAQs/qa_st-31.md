# ST-31 FAQ


## ST31-002 — Jinbe

**Q:** Can I use this [On Play] effect to play "ST31-005 Thousand Sunny"?

**A:** Yes, you can.


## ST31-003 — Brook

**Q:** Does the condition "if you have a total of 3 or more given DON!! cards" count both the DON!! cards given to this Character and the DON!! cards given to my other Characters and Leader?

**A:** Yes, it counts both the DON!! cards given to this Character and the ones given to your other Characters and Leader.

**Q:** My opponent declared an attack when I had a total of 3 or more given DON!! cards. They then used their [When Attacking] effect to remove another of my Characters with given DON!! cards from the field, causing my total number of given DON!! cards to drop to 2 or less. Can I still activate this Character's [Blocker] to block that attack?

**A:** No, you cannot.
