# ST-34 FAQ


## ST34-001 — Charlotte Katakuri

**Q:** Imagine that I have two "ST34-001 Charlotte Katakuri" and 10 DON!! cards on my field during my turn. I return 1 DON!! card to my DON!! deck, and activate the effect of one of my "ST34-001 Charlotte Katakuri" to add 1 DON!! card from my DON!! deck and rest it. Can I then use another card's effect to return 1 of the DON!! cards on my field to my DON!! deck, activate the effect of the other "ST34-001 Charlotte Katakuri", and add 1 more DON!! card from my DON!! deck and rest it?

**A:** No, you cannot.


## ST34-002 — Charlotte Cracker

**Q:** Can still I use this [On Play] effect to add 1 DON!! card from my DON!! deck and rest it if my opponent has no Characters with a cost of 2 or less on their field?

**A:** Yes, you can.

**Q:** If I have no cards in my DON!! deck, can I still use this [On Play] effect to K.O. 1 of my opponent's Characters with a cost of 2 or less?

**A:** Yes, you can.


## ST34-004 — Charlotte Linlin

**Q:** If an opponent's Character selected by this [On Play] effect has its base power changed by another effect, such as an effect that sets its base power to 6000, what will that Character's base power be?

**A:** In this case, among the effects applied to the same Character that change its base power, the effect that changes its base power to the highest value is applied. For example, if an effect that sets the Character's base power to 0 and an effect that sets its base power to 6000 are both applied to the same Character, its base power will be 6000.

**Q:** Can I still play this card and activate its [On Play] effect if this card is the only card in my hand?

**A:** No, you cannot. In this case, you will also be unable to DON!! −4, and will be unable to resolve the "Add up to 1 card from the top of your deck to the top of your Life cards. Then, up to 1 of your opponent's Characters' base power becomes 0 during this turn." part of the effect.
