# OP17 FAQ


## OP17-001 — Edward.Newgate

**Q:** If I have 0 cards in my hand, can I give up to 1 of my Leader or Character cards +4000 power during this battle with this [On Your Opponent's Attack] effect?

**A:** No, you cannot.


## OP17-005 — Edward.Newgate

**Q:** What does “your monocolored Leader” refer to?

**A:** It refers to a Leader that has only 1 color and does not have 2 or more colors.


## OP17-008 — Jozu

**Q:** If the base power of my Leader [Edward.Newgate] has been changed to 7000 by another effect that states “set its base power to 7000,” and I activate this [On Play] effect, what will my Leader's base power be?

**A:** In this case, its base power will be 8000 until the end of your opponent's next End Phase.


## OP17-010 — Fossa

**Q:** If my Leader has the effect “This Leader is treated as a card with all card names, types, and attributes.”, does this Character gain [Blocker] and +2000 power with this [Activate: Main] effect?

**A:** No. In this case, this Character cannot gain [Blocker] and does not gain +2000 power.


## OP17-015 — Marco

**Q:** If this Character and another one of my Characters, Character A, would be K.O.'d at the same time by an opponent's effect, can I use this Character's effect to K.O. this Character instead?

**A:** Yes, you can. In this case, only this Character is K.O.'d, and Character A remains on the field.

**Q:** If this Character would be returned to my hand or placed at the bottom of my deck by an opponent's effect, can I K.O. this Character instead?

**A:** Yes, you can.


## OP17-020 — Shanks

**Q:** If I have no cards in my hand, can I rest 1 of my DON!! cards with this [Activate: Main] effect?

**A:** Yes, you can. In this case, by resting 1 of your DON!! cards, you can apply the effect “Up to 1 of your opponent's rested Characters will not become active in your opponent's next Refresh Phase.”


## OP17-021 — Crone Oli

**Q:** If this Character itself would leave the field, can I rest 1 of my cards instead?

**A:** Yes, you can.

**Q:** Can I rest this Character itself as the card to be rested instead with this effect?

**A:** Yes, you can.

**Q:** If 2 of my Characters with a type including "Red-Haired Pirates" would leave the field at the same time, do I have to rest 2 cards?

**A:** No. In this case, rest 1 of your cards, and neither Character leaves the field.


## OP17-023 — Nami

**Q:** If this Character itself would be K.O.'d, can I rest this Character instead?

**A:** Yes, you can.

**Q:** If 2 of my Characters with the {East Blue} or {Straw Hat Crew} type would be K.O.'d at the same time, can I rest this Character so that neither of them is K.O.'d?

**A:** Yes, you can.


## OP17-034 — Rockstar

**Q:** If my opponent's Leader has 5000 power or less, can my Leader with the {Red-Haired Pirates} type have its base power set to 6000 until the end of my opponent's next End Phase?

**A:** No, you cannot.


## OP17-039 — Rocks.D.Xebec

**Q:** What happens to the card revealed with this [When Attacking] effect?

**A:** If the revealed card has a type including "Rocks Pirates", draw 2 cards from the top of your deck, including the revealed card. If the revealed card does not have a type including "Rocks Pirates", return it face-down to the top of your deck.


## OP17-040 — Edward.Newgate

**Q:** During my opponent's turn, when my Leader with a type including "Rocks Pirates" is attacked, which do I activate first: this [Once Per Turn] effect or my [Blocker] or Counter?

**A:** This “when attacked” effect activates at the same timing as your [On Your Opponent's Attack] effects, before activating your [Blocker] or Counter.


## OP17-043 — Ganzui

**Q:** If my Leader's base power has been changed to 7000 by another card's effect that states something like “set your Leader's base power to 7000,” what happens when this [On Play] effect is activated?

**A:** In this case, the effect that sets the base power to 7000 takes priority.


## OP17-044 — Captain John

**Q:** If I have this rested Character and a rested “P-067 Eustass"Captain"Kid” on my field, and my Leader has a type including "Rocks Pirates" or has the effect “This Leader is treated as a card with all card names, types, and attributes.”, can my opponent attack?

## OP17-047 — Shiki

**Q:** With this [End of Your Turn] effect, which player chooses the card to be placed at the bottom of the deck?

**A:** The opponent of the player who activated this [End of Your Turn] effect chooses 1 card from their own hand and places it at the bottom of their deck.


## OP17-049 — Charlotte Linlin

**Q:** If my opponent chooses “• Draw 2 cards.” with this [On Play] effect, which player draws the cards?

**A:** In this case, the player who activated this [On Play] effect draws 2 cards.

**Q:** If my opponent chooses “• Your opponent trashes 2 cards from their hand.” with this [On Play] effect, which player chooses the cards to be trashed?

**A:** The opponent of the player who activated this [On Play] effect chooses 2 cards from their own hand and trashes them.

**Q:** If my opponent has 1 or fewer cards in their hand when this [On Play] effect is activated, can they choose “• Your opponent trashes 2 cards from their hand.”?

**A:** Yes, they can. In this case, if your opponent has 1 card in their hand, they trash that card. If they have 0 cards in their hand, nothing happens.

## OP17-079 — Monkey.D.Luffy

**Q:** If this Leader's effect is negated by another effect such as “negate its effects,” do my Characters with a cost of 12 or more lose [Blocker] while that effect is negated?

**A:** Yes, they do.

**Q:** If one of my Characters with a cost of 12 or more has gained [Blocker] from this Leader's effect, and that Character is affected by another effect such as “negate its effects,” can that Character activate [Blocker]?

**A:** In this case, if the Character affected by the “negate its effects” effect still has a cost of 12 or more, it can activate [Blocker]. However, if an effect such as “This Character gains +12 cost.” is negated and its cost therefore becomes lower than 12, it loses [Blocker] while that effect is negated.

## OP17-099 — Charlotte Linlin

**Q:** After activating this [When Attacking] effect and trashing 1 card from my hand, if my opponent chooses “• Trash 1 card from your hand. Then, add up to 1 card from the top of your deck to the top of your Life cards.”, which player trashes a card from their hand?

**A:** The player who activated this [When Attacking] effect trashes 1 additional card from their hand, then adds up to 1 card from the top of their deck to the top of their Life cards.

**Q:** If I have 1 card in my hand and activate this [When Attacking] effect by trashing that card, then my opponent chooses “• Trash 1 card from your hand. Then, add up to 1 card from the top of your deck to the top of your Life cards.”, what happens?

**A:** In this case, since you have no cards in your hand when resolving “• Trash 1 card from your hand. Then, add up to 1 card from the top of your deck to the top of your Life cards.”, you do not trash a card and add up to 1 card from the top of your deck to the top of your Life cards.

**Q:** After activating this [When Attacking] effect and trashing 1 card from my hand, if my opponent chooses “• Trash 1 card from your opponent's hand.”, how is the card to be trashed chosen?

**A:** In this case, the player who activated this [When Attacking] effect randomly chooses 1 card from their opponent's hand without looking at it, and the opponent trashes that card.
