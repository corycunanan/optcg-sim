# ST-33 FAQ


## ST33-001 — Koby

**Q:** I play this card and activate its [On Play] effect when I only have this card in my hand. Can I draw 1 card without trashing 1 card from my hand?

**A:** No, you cannot.


## ST33-002 — Sakazuki

**Q:** If I have no cards in my hand, can I use this [When Attacking] effect to make my opponent trash 1 card from their hand without trashing 1 from mine?

**A:** No, you cannot.

**Q:** If my opponent has 5 or less cards in their hand, can I still use this card's [When Attacking] effect to trash 1 card from my hand?

**A:** Yes, you can. In this case, since your opponent does not have 6 or more cards in their hand, your opponent will not trash any cards from their hand.


## ST33-003 — Smoker

**Q:** I play this card and activate its [On Play] effect when I only have this card in my hand. Can I place up to 2 of my opponent's Characters with a cost of 2 or less at the bottom of the owner's deck without trashing 1 card from my hand?

**A:** No, you cannot.


## ST33-004 — Borsalino

**Q:** If a card in my hand is trashed by my opponent's effect, will this card in my hand be given −3 cost during that turn?

**A:** Yes, it will be given −3 cost.
