# ST-32 FAQ


## ST32-003 — Dracule Mihawk

**Q:** Can I use this [On Play] effect to play a Character card with a cost of 6 or more?

**A:** No, you cannot. This effect allows you to either play up to 1 [Perona] with a cost of 5 or less from your hand, or up to 1 Character card with a cost of 5 or less and the <Slash> attribute from your hand.


## ST32-005 — Roronoa Zoro

**Q:** Can I use this Character to attack Characters during my first turn?

**A:** No, you cannot.
