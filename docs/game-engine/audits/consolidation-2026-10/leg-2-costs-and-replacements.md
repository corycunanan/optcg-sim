# Leg 2 — Costs, replacements, prohibitions

ORCHESTRATOR CORRECTION (2026-10-09): the Koby OP11-001 "reachable bug" is WRONG. Scope: `PROPOSAL-REV3.md` (Rev 3) against `docs/rules/rule_comprehensive.md`. Code evidence is from static reading of `workers/game/src` at the checkout in this session. No tests were run and no repo files were edited.

## Verdict

The proposal handles the cost-availability and sequential-feasibility problem (OPT-864) and the replacement-recheck problem (OPT-922) well, and it cites §8-3-1-7 correctly. It never addresses prohibitions (§1-3-3, §8-2-1-1), the printed-order rule for replacement precedence (§8-1-3-4-2), or area overflow (§3-7-6-1), and it does not state the mid-payment outcome of §8-3-1-3-1. The most serious current-code problem sits outside the proposal's named tickets: the Koby (OP11-001) replacement can save a Character when its substitute cannot be paid, which breaks §8-1-3-4-5 and the OP11-001 FAQ.

## Recommended fixture additions

1. Koby OP11-001 with 2 trash cards and a Navy Character of 7000 or less removed by an opponent effect: no replacement prompt, and the Character is removed (§8-1-3-4-5; `qa_op11.md` OP11-001 Q2).

4. Turn player's optional replacement and non-turn player's mandatory replacement, both eligible: turn player's replacement is ordered first (§8-1-3-4-2).
5. Replacement whose substitute makes a second replacement eligible: the second replacement is offered (§8-1-3-4-6).
6. Single-target KO with two eligible replacements: declining the first offers the second (§8-1-3-4-1; OPT-883).

8. Substitute raises the same event on the same target: the replacement is not applied again (§8-1-3-4-3).
9. Replaced activation cost: earlier costs stay paid, and the effect after the colon does not resolve (§8-3-1-7).
10. Cost sequence where the first cost enables the second (for example, rest DON!! then a cost that needs a rested DON!!): the activation is offered (§8-3-1-1; OPT-864).
11. Same sequence as fixture 10, used as a Life Trigger cost: the Trigger is offered (§8-3-1-1; `effect-damage.ts:96-98`).
12. Mid-payment inability (a forced test with an earlier cost paid): paid costs stay paid, the effect does not resolve, and once-per-turn consumption follows the ruling (§8-3-1-3-1; §10-2-13-5).
13. Prohibited `ACTIVATE_EFFECT` with a payable cost: availability reports it unavailable (§1-3-3; §8-2-1-1).
14. Replacement decline does not pay the replacement's cost (§8-1-3-4-1).
15. 6th Character played by an effect while a second play frame is pending: the controller trashes one of their own Characters, no On K.O. fires, and both plays finish (§3-7-6-1; §3-7-6-1-1).
16. Stage overflow by an effect-driven play (§3-8-5-1).
17. "From your field" DON!! return with attached DON on a Character: attached DON is in the pool (§3-1-2; OPT-921).
18. DON!! −X with attached DON on the Leader and Characters: attached DON counts and returns to the DON!! deck (§10-2-10-1; §8-3-1-6).
19. Character with given DON!! leaves the field under control change: DON goes to the cost area of the player who gave it, rested (§6-5-5-4; needs ruling).
20. [DON!! xX] with DON!! given before the condition was first met (§10-2-9-1; §8-3-2-3).
21. Two effects add costs to one activation: costs are paid in resolving order (§8-3-1-2).
