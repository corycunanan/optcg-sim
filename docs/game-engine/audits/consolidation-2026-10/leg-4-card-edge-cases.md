# Leg 4 — Documented card edge cases

Paths are relative to `/Users/cory-cunanan/Projects/optcg-sim` unless absolute. Rule numbers come from the proposal and existing docs. I did not check them against `docs/rules/`. No tests were run.

## Verdict

The proposal's frame list and single scheduler cover most documented edge cases in this set, including the End Phase order, deck-out timing, sequential cost availability, and the replacement recheck. The gaps cluster in four areas the frame list does not name: battle-step windows (attack-target redirect and Counter-step plays), scheduled obligations bound to a card instance or to battle end, continuous zone-destination replacements (ST13-003), and deck-out as a replacement of the terminal outcome (OP03-040). Five rows need a named frame kind, a scheduler rule, or a ruling before the affected phase starts.

Row count: 31. HANDLED 19, NEEDS-FRAME 4, UNCLEAR 4 (rows 3, 7, 27, 31), EXCLUDED 4 (rows 19, 28, 29, 30).

## Patterns the proposal's frame-kind list does not name

1. **Battle-step windows.** Attack declaration, the redirect window, Block, and the Counter step live on `turn.battle` (rows 12, 13). Only battle damage is a named frame kind.
2. **Scheduled obligations outside End Phase.** Battle-end placement (row 16) and deferred actions bound to a played-card instance (row 15) are not named. `state.scheduledActions` (`workers/game/src/engine/duration-tracker.ts:208`) already stores scheduled work separately from frames.
3. **Trigger ordering choice.** Two watchers on the same removal (OP08-105 and OP12-099) need a player-ordering prompt. The proposal names `processPlayerTriggerGroup` only as an implementation inventory item (Phase 2). It names no frame kind for a pending ordering choice.
4. **Continuous zone-destination replacement.** ST13-003 (row 27) changes Life destinations for the whole game. It is not a per-event replacement frame.
5. **Terminal outcome replacement.** OP03-040 (row 3) replaces a loss with a win. `terminateGame` has no replacement step.
6. **Once-per-turn ledger.** `state.turn.oncePerTurnUsed` (`docs/game-engine/08-ENGINE-ARCHITECTURE.md`) is turn state, not a frame. Rows 19 and 23 depend on it surviving reload. Strict schema support must cover it.
7. **Pregame machine.** `pregame.phase`, `mulliganDecisions`, and the `PREGAME_*` prompt types (`workers/game/src/engine/pregame.ts`) form their own machine. Row 22 nests an effect inside it. Phase 1 names pregame only as an inventory item.
8. **Trigger staging marker.** `turn.triggerStagingInstanceIds` (OPT-257) hides the just-trashed Life card during a Trigger. It is state, not a frame, and must persist with the frame.
9. **Event activation from hand.** OP12-041, OP15-014, and OP15-046 activate an Event from hand inside On Play or Activate effects. The debt frame needs a source-zone field. OPT-236 defines three classes.
10. **Deferred loss flag.** Brook's end-of-turn loss (row 2) is a recorded condition. The phase-boundary stage must hold it.
11. **AND-group commit.** No frame kind or allowlist check covers it (row 31). The registry has zero uses today.

## Recommended acceptance fixtures

1. ST13-002 with OP08-105 and OP12-099: End Phase trashes Life in the ending player's wave. Watchers fire in order with turn gates checked at fire time.
2. OP15-022: deck hits 0 mid-turn with no loss. The loss applies at end of turn, and the flag survives a handoff.
3. OP03-040 with OP15-022 in play: the ruling decides precedence. The fixture asserts that ruling, once found.
4. EB01-051: paying a deck-emptying cost processes defeat before any further prompt.
5. OP07-079 with one deck card: optional activation is not offered.
6. OP14-115 at 0 Life after the add-to-Life: defeat is recorded and play stops.
7. EB03-055 with an opponent Trigger on the last Life: the Trigger resolves, then play continues.
8. OP06-116 with an opponent Trigger on the last Life: the Trigger resolves before the "Then add 1 card" step. Reproduce against the rules first.
9. Synthetic two-damage effect, first hit a Trigger: remainder follows the confirmed rule. Repeat with a reload at each prompt.
10. OP05-100 (two copies, one Life card, mass K.O.): paying the first copy removes the second's option.
11. ST25-003 with OP15-090, one card in hand: the second replacement is offered only if still feasible.
12. OP16-080: after paying the Trigger-card cost and redirecting, WHEN_ATTACKED fires for the new target only. Reload between cost and target choice.
13. OP02-045: a Counter-step Character with a K.O. On Play resolves nested, then battle damage resolves.
14. OP11-092: the played Character goes to the bottom at end of turn if still present. No played Character means no scheduled action.
15. OP02-064: the attacker goes to the bottom at end of battle only if still on the field.
16. OP16-103: a Trigger reusing the [On K.O.] block on the opponent's turn fires. The same Trigger on the player's own turn does nothing. Once-per-turn is keyed by block and instance.
17. OP13-079 with a reload before the Stage prompt is answered: opening hands deal after the Stage resolves.
18. OP13-119: if the return does not happen, the opponent does not play a Character.
19. P-098: when the condition fails, the Character goes to the bottom. The remaining chain holds no reference to the moved instance.
20. EB03-031: the Event stays in trash, its printed cost is skipped, and its [Main] resolves once.
