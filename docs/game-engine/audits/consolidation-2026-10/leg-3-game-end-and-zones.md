# Leg 3 — Game end, rule processing, zones, visibility

ORCHESTRATOR CORRECTION (2026-10-09): finding #4 (Double Attack into 1 Life wins) is WRONG. OPT-886 (PR #721, `bfb9d8d7`) is on the baseline; `pipeline.test.ts:669` and `opt-239-double-attack-damage-split.test.ts:353` assert that the defender does not lose. The verdict sentence "gives Double Attack at 1 Life a win that the repo FAQ denies" is withdrawn. Findings on `checkDefeat` placement and the OPT-925 damaged-player flag were re-verified and hold.

## Verdict

The proposal has the right direction for terminal cleanup, source snapshots, and visibility, but it never says when defeat is judged. Current source judges defeat once per action, drops the damage flag for effect damage, and gives Double Attack at 1 Life a win that the repo FAQ denies. Phase 0B needs one joint, named rule-processing checkpoint and three fixtures before "stop when status is not IN_PROGRESS" is safe.
