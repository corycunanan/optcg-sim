RESPONSIBILITY CLASSIFICATION (source observations, not migration-policy decisions)
SAME-EFFECT CONTINUATION: cost payment/branch/arrangement and abort; target/overflow/Don identity; deck/search/Life arrangement; optional/action/value/branch choices; executeActionChain and batch suffix reentry. These own action-local progress/resultRefs/payment and may return prompts. Their calls to processRemainingTriggers currently cross into scheduling; replacing a handler name alone does not remove that ownership.
CROSS-EFFECT SCHEDULING: resume/triggers.processRemainingTriggers groups by controller, invokes resolveEffect, prompts for order, then reenters batches; trigger-queue.processPlayerTriggerGroup and processTriggerQueuePipeline maintain alternate pipeline execution. choice.handleAwaitingTriggerOrderSelection both consumes ordering and executes selected/nested effects. The new trigger-executor advances generations and reports newly fulfilled work into the collector; its Life helper stops at an exact damage boundary.
PUBLICATION/SCANNING: resume/events helpers transfer event ownership and publication flags (not independent executable queues). scanEventsForTriggers matches work but also updates registrations/timestamps/propagation; retain this distinction from activation. The facade services in resolver.ts336/345 convert cross-effect drains to reports only when a v5 owner already exists. Normal producers remain old behavior.
