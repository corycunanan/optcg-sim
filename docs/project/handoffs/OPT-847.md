## Contract and files to read first

Read `engine/effect-source.ts`, `effect-resolver/actions/play.ts`, `replacements.ts`, `triggers.ts`, and `__tests__/opt-847-rest-fidelity.test.ts` (all under `workers/game/src`). SET_REST projects the existing `EFFECT_SOURCE_SNAPSHOT_REF` pre-payment CardInstance into `{instanceId, cardId, cardType}` rather than creating another snapshot contract. This identity is preserved in the replacement-batch continuation and finalized rest event. Thus a Character trashed as its cost remains a Character cause even after its old instance is absent. Real Monet plus Helmeppo cost reduction exercises that for Zoro; Monet alone exercises Buffalo.

`CARD_STATE_CHANGED` and its strict persisted validator now accept optional causingSource and `EFFECT_COST`. Both fixed REST_SELF and selected REST_CARDS/REST_NAMED_CARD costs publish effect-controller provenance; the latter reuses the frame's snapshot. Costs remain COST for prohibition checks. Broad trigger matching counts activation costs per Bartolomeo's official FAQ. Attack/block declarations remain without effect provenance. Legacy persisted events load; malformed new source identities reject. Unknown/missing source kind cannot satisfy a Character-source gate.

Rest events are public (`engine/visibility.ts`). Producers identify the publicly activated card effect, including Events played/revealed to activate them, and publish no source snapshot, hand contents, Life contents, or transient statistics. Session persistence validates the new payload; the app sandbox's event reducer continues reading only subject/state. No hidden card choice is exposed by this identity projection.

No-op rest is removed before replacement interception, so it cannot offer or pay a Zoro substitute. Zoro's alternate target filter explicitly requires active state, and existing SET_REST prohibition filtering plus replacement feasibility enforce the official legal-alternate FAQ.

## Independent review correction — DON targets

External P1 [discussion_r4002797557](https://github.com/corycunanan/optcg-sim/pull/669#discussion_r4002797557) confirmed two distinct facts: OPT-847's new no-op filter dropped every DON ID because `findCardInstance` excludes the DON cost area; the earlier `setCardState` implementation also did not mutate DON, so removing that filter alone would restore false success. Real registered Judge (both controllers) and Viola cost/target sequences failed three tests at the reviewed head. Temporarily removing only the filter still failed the same three final-state assertions (`847-don-red.log`, `847-don-prefilter-red.log`).

The narrow correction centralizes ordinary and replacement-decline rest finalization in `engine/field-rest.ts`. Active field cards retain Character-rest provenance; active cost-area DON are updated immutably and publish DON_STATE_CHANGED for their controller, never CARD_STATE_CHANGED. Rested, missing, and hidden-area instances do not produce rest events or success. Zoro's no-op exclusion remains intact. The helper is shared with persisted replacement finalization so the two paths cannot silently diverge again. Selection constraints and source eligibility remain owned by the existing target resolver.

The expanded suite has 59 passing tests: Judge both controllers and DON-minus payment, Viola two-DON payment, active/rested DON with Bartolomeo/Perona present, illegal own/hidden/missing target rejection, original Character/Zoro coverage, and preservation of EB03-012/061's eligible Character alternatives. Six affected suites passed 137 tests before the final additional rejection test; worker types and focused ESLint passed (existing underscore-parameter warnings only). Full renewed verification and exact integrated SHA are recorded in the PR body.
