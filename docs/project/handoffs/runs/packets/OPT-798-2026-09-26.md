## Ticket (verbatim)

**Engine: MILL cost primitive and either-player Character-to-deck cost — five "you may trash N from top of deck:" cards and two "place 1 Character…owner's deck" costs are mis-encoded**

### Problem
1. No `MILL` cost exists, so pre-colon "You may trash N cards from the top of your deck:" is authored as the first resolving action: EB01-051 Finger Pistol, OP07-079 Rob Lucci, EB04-042 Alpha, EB04-049 Finger Pistol Yellow Lotus, OP15-088 (which additionally has a **bogus** `TRASH_FROM_HAND` ×3 cost). Effects resolve even when the deck cannot pay, and availability/feasibility reads are wrong.
2. `PLACE_OWN_CHARACTER_TO_DECK` is own-only, but OP04-055 Plague Rounds and OP06-043 Aramaki say "place 1 Character with a cost of N or less at the bottom of the owner's deck" (either player's).

### Fix
- Add `MILL` to `Cost` (amount, optional) with feasibility = deck size ≥ amount; re-author the five cards (remove OP15-088's hand-trash cost).
- Add `controller: "EITHER"` support to `PLACE_OWN_CHARACTER_TO_DECK` (or a new `PLACE_CHARACTER_TO_OWNER_DECK` cost); re-author OP04-055, OP06-043.
- Lint: a `MILL` action as the first action of an `activate`/`MAIN_EVENT` block whose source text has "trash N cards from the top of your deck:" before the colon is an error.

### Acceptance
- EB01-051 is unavailable with a 1-card deck; OP15-088 no longer discards from hand.
- OP06-043 can bottom-deck an opponent's cost ≤2 Character as its cost.
- Worker tests; `pnpm schema:check` green.
