# OPT-900 handoff

Issue: https://linear.app/optcg-sim/issue/OPT-900/op07-057-perfume-femur-is-encoded-as-grant-keyword-unblockable-instead

## Sources and behavior

`docs/cards/OP-07.md:410`: “[Main] Select up to 1 of your {The Seven Warlords of the Sea} type Leader or Character cards and that card gains +2000 power during this turn. Then, if the selected card attacks during this turn, your opponent cannot activate [Blocker].” Trigger draws one card.

- “Select up to 1” permits zero; Leader and Character are both eligible.
- “the selected card” binds the prohibition to the same instance selected for +2000 power.
- “your opponent cannot activate [Blocker]” is an opponent prohibition, with no keyword grant.
- “during this turn” expires both power and prohibition at turn end.
- `docs/FAQs/qa_op07.md` has no OP07-057 entry. Analogous `qa_st-21.md` ST21-003 and `qa_st-01-st-04.md` ST01-016 explicitly allow Blocker against another attacker after the selected card attacks.
- `docs/rules/rule_comprehensive.md` §7-1-2-1 and §10-1-4-1: Blocker activation belongs to the current battle's Block Step.
